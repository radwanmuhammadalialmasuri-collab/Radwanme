# Privacy Policy

We put your privacy and data security as our top priority:

1. **Camera Usage:**
   The app requests camera access for one single purpose: performing instant scanning of QR codes and barcodes through the screen.

2. **On-Device Data Processing:**
   Images and codes are processed entirely locally on your phone device without sending any images or videos to any external server.

3. **No Data Collection or Storage:**
   The app does not record any personal data, store images, or track your geographical location.

4. **Total Security:**
   The app is designed to work completely securely and at super-fast speed without needing any unnecessary permissions.
