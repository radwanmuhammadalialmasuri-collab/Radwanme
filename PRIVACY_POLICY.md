# Privacy Policy for Web Page Reader & Text Cleaner

**Last updated:** October 7, 2026

## Overview
**Web Page Reader & Text Cleaner** ("we," "our," or "the extension") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our browser extension.

---

## Data Collection and Usage

### No Personal Data Collection
We do **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, or personal data. 

### Local Processing Only
All operations performed by the extension—including text reading, formatting removal, and clipboard interactions—occur entirely **on-device** within your local browser environment. No text content processed by the extension is ever uploaded to external servers or transmitted to third parties.

---

## Browser Permissions

To deliver its core features, the extension requests the following minimum necessary permissions:

* **`clipboardRead`**: Used solely to retrieve copied text from your system clipboard when you choose to process or clean text within the extension interface.
* **`clipboardWrite`**: Used solely to copy processed and cleaned text back to your system clipboard at your request.
* **`activeTab`**: Used strictly to interact with the text elements on the active webpage you are currently viewing to enable text cleaning and reading functionality.
* **`scripting`**: Required to execute client-side scripts to process formatting and adjust text visual layout locally.

---

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs.

---

## Data Security
Since no data leaves your local browser, your information remains fully protected within your own device environment.
